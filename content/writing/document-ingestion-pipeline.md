The idea is to have a central place to upload any form of data: Websites, CSVs, PDFs, XMLs, Images, Videos (youtube videos). We'll have a knowledge codec to convert unstructured data into easy to parse sources.

CAP

- Consistent
- Available
- Partition tolerant

NFRs

- Hold up to 1M RPS and 100k WPS

Deliverables:

1. Storage
2. Ingestion pipeline
3. Retrieval pipeline
4. MCP to ingest and retrieve information
5. High-level architecture
