An applied-research exploration: train and probe a CLIP-based **Visual
Natural Language Autoencoder**, extending the [NLA technique][paper]
([overview][post]) from language-model activations to image embeddings.

The aim is a credible publishable artifact — a written explainer and an
open-source training and probing [repo][repo]. A hosted demo will follow
once the model is trained.

Entries log the daily decisions, dead-ends, and small wins as the work
moves from "read the paper" to "shipped artifact."
